## Review focus

- Cancellation during restore inspection/publication: decrypted stage must be removed without deleting an active preview.
- Reboot, stale alarm, past time, and colliding reminder IDs: no notification from ineligible sources; restore replaces rather than duplicates alarms.
- Renamed Random Links across old/new backup restoration: routing and memberships survive without duplicate special collections.
- Rapid query/filter/privacy changes during suspension: older results/evidence must not publish or upload after invalidation.
- Edited or excluded media: local keyword text remains searchable and ordinary questions never reupload originals.
